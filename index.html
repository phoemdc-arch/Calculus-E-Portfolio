Learning Journey & Reflections

01

The Bridge Between Calculus and Cybersecurity

Core Course Synthesis

Throughout 2CALC-IT, I discovered that mathematical concepts like limits, derivatives, and integrals are far from abstract theory—they are foundational to IT and Cybersecurity. Limits model asymptotic throughputs and network bottleneck capacities; derivatives measure instantaneous rates of traffic change and anomaly detection bursts; integrals quantify cumulative bandwidth consumption and storage accumulation.

02

Perseverance and Analytical Growth

Overcoming Challenges

Working through practice sets, drills, quizzes, exams, and group projects taught me the value of step-by-step problem-solving and perseverance. Every challenge—from low quiz scores to complex chain rule calculations—served as a stepping stone toward analytical confidence.
